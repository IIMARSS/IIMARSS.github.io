## Research Overview

This project investigates hyperspectral characterization and recognition of insulator contamination under natural illumination and varying observation distances. It combines outdoor data acquisition, spectral analysis, and deep learning to study contamination-related information under changing observation conditions.

## My Contribution

I design outdoor hyperspectral experiments and datasets under varying illumination, weather, and observation-distance conditions. I develop and evaluate methods for spectral-spatial representation learning, contamination-level recognition, hyperspectral unmixing, and lightweight inference.

## Related Work

Published work includes the Fast contamination recognition network. Related manuscripts under review address amplitude-shape analysis across sunny and cloudy conditions, observation-distance effects and effective distance estimation, recurrent endmember representation learning, and SpecRetNet.
