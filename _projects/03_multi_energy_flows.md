## Research Overview

This research topic forms part of a National Key Research and Development Program of China project. It concerns the modeling, simulation, and interaction analysis of electricity, hydrogen, and thermal energy flows in transportation hubs.

## My Contribution

I contributed to proposal preparation and technical roadmap design. My ongoing work involves developing coupled models and data-driven methods to characterize energy transmission, conversion, and interactions across multiple timescales.
