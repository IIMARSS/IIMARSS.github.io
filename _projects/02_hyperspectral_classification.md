**Period:** September 2020 – June 2023  
**Role:** Master's research  
**Institution / program:** Wuhan Polytechnic University  
**Supervisor:** Prof. Shan Zeng

## Research Overview

This project studies 3D convolutional approaches to hyperspectral image classification, with a focus on integrating spectral attention, multi-scale convolutions, and spatial-spectral feature fusion.

## My Contribution

I designed and implemented the classification models and conducted ablation, parameter, and comparative experiments on multiple benchmark datasets. I also prepared the resulting journal publications.

## Related Work

The project produced A Novel Spatial–Spectral Pyramid Network for Hyperspectral Image Classification (IEEE TGRS, 2023) and An enhanced spectral fusion 3D CNN model for hyperspectral image classification (Remote Sensing, 2022).
