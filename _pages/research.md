My research brings together hyperspectral image processing, deep learning, and engineering condition assessment. I study how spectral and spatial information can be represented, interpreted, and used under practical acquisition and computational constraints.

## Spectral-Spatial Representation Learning

I develop deep learning methods that jointly exploit spectral information and spatial context in hyperspectral images. My work includes 3D convolutional networks, spectral attention, multi-scale feature fusion, and retention-based representations for image classification.

**Related work:** Spatial-Spectral Pyramid Network; Enhanced Spectral Fusion 3D CNN; HSRNet (accepted).

## Hyperspectral Unmixing and Super-Resolution

I investigate recurrent representations for hyperspectral analysis and reconstruction. My ongoing work includes recurrent endmember representation learning for hyperspectral unmixing and spectral state propagation for hyperspectral super-resolution.

**Related manuscripts:** ERRNet; Recurrent Spectral State Propagation for Hyperspectral Image Super-Resolution. Both are under review.

## Outdoor Hyperspectral Inspection

I study hyperspectral characterization and recognition of insulator contamination under natural illumination and varying observation distances. This research combines outdoor experiments with analyses of weather-related spectral changes, spatial-spectral degradation, effective observation distance, and contamination recognition under low irradiance and extended standoff.

**Related work:** doctoral research on insulator contamination; amplitude-shape analysis, observation-distance effects, and SpecRetNet (manuscripts under review).

## Efficient Deep Learning

I investigate knowledge distillation and lightweight inference for hyperspectral recognition. This work focuses on transferring useful representations to compact models for outdoor insulator contamination assessment.

**Related publication:** Fast contamination recognition network.
