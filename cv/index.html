Research Experience

Outdoor Hyperspectral Characterization and Recognition of Insulator Contamination
September 2023 – Present · Doctoral research
I design outdoor hyperspectral experiments and datasets under varying illumination, weather, and observation-distance conditions. I develop and evaluate methods for spectral-spatial representation learning, contamination-level recognition, hyperspectral unmixing, and lightweight inference.

Hyperspectral Image Classification with 3D Convolutional Neural Networks
September 2020 – June 2023 · Master’s research
I designed and implemented the classification models and conducted ablation, parameter, and comparative experiments on multiple benchmark datasets. I also prepared the resulting journal publications.

Interaction Characteristics and Analysis Methods for Electricity, Hydrogen, and Thermal Multi-Energy Flows
June 2025 – Present · Project core member
I contributed to proposal preparation and technical roadmap design. My ongoing work involves developing coupled models and data-driven methods to characterize energy transmission, conversion, and interactions across multiple timescales.
